Widgets

yAI on your home screen: a daily brief with your weather, LEARN due dates, the latest scheduled task and your PC's Remote status. Phones only allow widgets from installed apps, so it's drawn by a free widget app that reads your private brief link.

1 · Your brief

Pick the place for the weather, then make your private link. Anyone with the link can read the brief (not your chats), so keep it to yourself; you can renew or turn it off any time.

2 · iPhone: Scriptable

  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store (free).
  2. In Scriptable, tap +, paste the script below (it already has your link), and name it yAI.
  3. On your home screen, long-press → + → Scriptable → pick a small or medium widget → tap it → Script: yAI.

Shortcuts

            
Install yAI as an app (account menu → Install the app), then long-press its icon for New chat, Ask by voice, Remote and Scheduled. Links like /?q=what's due this week open yAI and ask straight away, handy for iOS Shortcuts or a launcher.
